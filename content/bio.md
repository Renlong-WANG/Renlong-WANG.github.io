I am the first-year Ph.D. student in Civil Engineering at [Rice University](https://www.rice.edu/about), where I will work with Dr. [Jamie Padgett](https://profiles.rice.edu/faculty/jamie-padgett). I recently completed my M.S. in Industrial Engineering and Management at the [University of Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://english.ucas.ac.cn/index.php/about-ucas/introduction) (UCAS), advised by Dr. [Mingang Gao](https://people.ucas.ac.cn/~0028787). I received my B.Eng. in Civil Engineering from [Nanjing Tech University](https://en.njtech.edu.cn/About/General_Information.htm) in 2023, advised by Dr. [Lingzhi Li](https://cce.njtech.edu.cn/info/1098/3647.htm).

My research aims to develop innovative methods for smart and equitable infrastructure resilience. I am especially interested in advancing infrastructure resilience research from modeling analytics to predictive analytics, and ultimately to prescriptive analytics, integrating simulation, prediction, and operations research within a unified decision-making framework. Rather than treating these approaches in isolation, my work seeks to enable them to interact synergistically to better understand, anticipate, and manage infrastructure resilience. Within this framework, I study how interdependent infrastructure systems can be effectively managed and restored when decisions are distributed across multiple stakeholders operating under uncertainty and resource limitations.

A more detailed overview of my past and ongoing work is available on my [Research](/research) page.
